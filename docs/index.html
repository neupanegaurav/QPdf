The toolkit

A full PDF workspace, not a viewer with a paywall.

The tools professionals reach for — markup, forms, page surgery, OCR and real redaction — in one app that never asks you to sign in.

Read & search

Fast page rendering with text selection, in-document search, thumbnails, bookmarks and an outline panel. Large documents stay smooth on phone, tablet and desktop.

Fill & sign

Type text anywhere on a page — scanned and flattened forms behave just like digital ones — then add a drawn signature, or apply and verify a cryptographic digital signature.

  • Type on page
  • Drawn signature
  • Digital signature
  • Verify

Annotate & markup

Highlight, underline, strike through, draw freehand, drop shapes, insert text and measure distances.

Organise pages

Reorder, rotate, extract, delete and merge. Add page numbers, Bates numbering or a watermark in one pass.

Scan & create

Capture paper with the camera or turn a batch of photos into a clean, shareable PDF.

OCR that never leaves the device

Add a selectable, searchable text layer to a scan. The recognition model is downloaded once (about 21 MB, checksum-verified) and every page after that is processed locally.

  • Searchable text layer
  • One-time model
  • No page uploads

Redaction that actually redacts

Secure redaction writes an image-only copy: searchable text, interactive content, metadata and existing signatures are removed, not just covered with a black box.

  • Image flattening
  • Metadata stripped

Questions

The things people ask first.

Everything here is also covered, in more detail and in plainer legal terms, in the privacy policy.

Is QPdf really free?
Yes. There is no subscription, no paid unlock and no watermark on exported files. Features are not gated behind a tier.
Do my documents get uploaded anywhere?
No. Rendering, search, annotation, editing, forms, signing, page operations, scanning and OCR all run on your device. Document content is never sent to us or to a third party.
Then why does OCR need the network?
Only to fetch the text-recognition model the first time you use it — roughly 21 MB over HTTPS, verified against a known SHA-256 checksum before use. Your document is not part of that request, and recognition itself happens locally.
Do I need an account?
No. There is no sign-up, no profile and no tracking identifier, so there is nothing to log in to and nothing for us to hold about you.
Is the redaction tool safe to rely on?
Secure redaction produces an image-only copy of the document and removes searchable text, interactive content, metadata and existing signatures — rather than drawing a black rectangle over text that can still be copied.
What happens to my edits if the app crashes?
While you have unsaved changes, QPdf keeps a local recovery journal so your work survives a crash or a force-quit. It is deleted as soon as you save, export or discard those changes.
Which permissions does it ask for?
Camera, only while you are scanning, and photos or files, only when you pick something to open. Decline either one and the rest of the app keeps working.
