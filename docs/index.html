QPdf

A complete PDF workspace that keeps your documents on your device.

Read, annotate, fill forms, sign, scan, OCR and redact — free, with no account, no subscription and no watermark.

What it does

  • Read & searchFast rendering, text selection, in-document search
  • AnnotateHighlight, underline, strike through, draw, add notes
  • Fill & signInteractive forms, drawn and cryptographic signatures
  • Organise pagesReorder, rotate, extract, delete, merge, number
  • CreateBuild PDFs from photos, or scan paper with the camera
  • OCRAdd a searchable text layer entirely on-device
  • RedactSecure image-only output that strips hidden text
  • AuditPDF/UA accessibility and PDF/A archiving checks

Private by design. No account. No advertising SDK. No analytics SDK. QPdf does not upload the PDFs you open. See the privacy policy for the detail.
