Design Services Agreement

Between Meridian Design Studio and Harbourline Coffee Roasters

1. Scope of work

Meridian Design Studio (“the Studio”) agrees to provide brand identity and packaging design services to Harbourline Coffee Roasters (“the Client”) as set out in this agreement. The engagement covers primary logotype development, a supporting colour and typographic system, and artwork for three retail packaging formats.

Work begins on 30 March 2026 and is scheduled across four stages, each closing with a written approval from the Client before the following stage opens. Stage approvals are recorded in the project log and are treated as final for the purposes of this agreement.

3. Revisions

Each stage includes two rounds of revision. Further rounds, or changes that reopen a stage already approved in writing, are billed at the Studio’s standard hourly rate of 140.00 per hour and are agreed in advance with the Client.

4. Intellectual property

On receipt of final payment for all stages, the Studio assigns to the Client the full rights in the approved logotype, colour system and packaging artwork. Concepts presented but not selected remain the property of the Studio. The Studio retains the right to display the completed work in its portfolio and in award submissions unless the Client requests otherwise in writing.

5. Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep commercial information disclosed during the engagement confidential, and not to share it with a third party without written consent, except where disclosure is required by law. This obligation continues for two years after the engagement ends.

6. Termination

Either party may end this agreement with 14 days’ written notice. On termination the Client pays for all work completed and in progress up to the notice date, and the Studio delivers the working files for that work within ten business days.

7. Acceptance

By signing below, both parties confirm they have read this agreement and agree to the terms set out in sections 1 to 6.
